# Aloha Growth: Podcast-Style Ad Scripts (for AI voice)

Each script is about 30 seconds when read aloud. Approve or edit every line before I generate the voice.
The founder line uses only facts from `founder-story-and-program.md`. The scripts don't promise funding,
timelines or results.

---

## Video A: First Location (home daycare to first center)

| # | Beat | Line | On-screen object (left / right of head) |
|---|------|------|------------------------------------------|
| 1 | Hook | "Running a home daycare and dreaming about your first real center? Stay with me." | House icon → building icon |
| 2 | Pain | "Most home providers get stuck in the same three places." | "3" counter |
| 3 | Pain | "Not enough capital. No idea where to find the right building. And no system to run it once you're in." | Dollar card / map pin / gear, one per phrase |
| 4 | Authority | "I grew my own company from one rented truck to an eight-figure business. The lesson: growth isn't about working harder. It's about systems." | Truck → "8 FIGURES" stat |
| 5 | Offer | "In our program we help you find the right location, build your capital plan, set up the business, and put the systems in place to scale it." | 4-step checklist ticking off |
| 6 | CTA | "Comment FIRST and I'll send you the first-location blueprint." | Comment bubble typing "FIRST" |

## Video B: Second Location (existing operators)

| # | Beat | Line | On-screen object |
|---|------|------|------------------|
| 1 | Hook | "Already running a daycare or preschool, and thinking about location number two? This is for you." | Pin 1 → Pin 2 |
| 2 | Pain | "Here's the trap. If your first center still depends on you, a second one doubles the chaos, not the profit." | "2× CHAOS" vs "2× PROFIT" toggle |
| 3 | Authority | "I grew my own company from one rented truck to an eight-figure business, and the second location is where most owners break." | Truck → "8 FIGURES" stat |
| 4 | Offer | "We step inside your business, find the real bottleneck, then help you secure the right site, plan the capital, and systemize operations, sales and marketing so location two runs without you." | Diagnostic scan → checklist |
| 5 | CTA | "Comment EXPAND and I'll send you the expansion blueprint." | Comment bubble typing "EXPAND" |
