# VSL Script (short cut): "One Rented Truck" (about 5 minutes)

> The full 7-minute shooting script is `05-shoot-script.md`. Use this file as the short version or for cutting a 4-5 minute edit.

**Audience:** daycare owners in South Jersey and Philadelphia doing $500K+ a year.
**Speaker:** the founder (HeyGen avatar). **Read pace:** about 150 words per minute, roughly 750 spoken words.
**Rules for this script:** every fact below came from the founder. Items in [BRACKETS] must be filled with real information or cut. Do not add results, testimonials or guarantees that aren't real and approved.

Format: `[TIME]` | **VISUAL / ON-SCREEN** | *SPOKEN WORDS*

---

### 1. HOOK (0:00-0:25)
**VISUAL:** Close-up on the speaker, direct to camera. A single line of kinetic text: "Your daycare depends on you for everything."
*"If you own a daycare, and your phone rings at 9 p.m. because a teacher called out, a parent is upset, or a bill is due, then you don't have a business yet. You have a very demanding job. I'm going to show you what changes that, and I'm going to start with a truck."*

### 2. PATTERN INTERRUPT: THE TRUCK (0:25-1:10)
**VISUAL:** B-roll of a single box truck at dawn. Year "2020" appears.
*"In 2020, I rented one truck. That was the entire company. About fourteen years earlier, I'd moved from Egypt to the United States. I didn't come from money. I had a finance degree and a lot of ideas. Transportation became the biggest of the businesses I started."*

### 3. THE NUMBERS (1:10-2:10)
**VISUAL:** Animated bar chart builds year by year: 2021 $220K, 2022 $340K, 2023 $3.4M, 2024 $11.5M, 2025 $16.7M. The 2022-to-2023 jump is highlighted in coral.
*"Two hundred twenty thousand dollars in 2021. Three hundred forty thousand in 2022. Then 2023, three point four million. 2024, eleven and a half million. 2025, sixteen point seven million. Look at that jump between 2022 and 2023. I didn't suddenly work ten times harder. I couldn't have. What changed was that I stopped running everything myself and started building a company that could run without me in every room."*

### 4. THE PROBLEM (2:10-3:20)
**VISUAL:** Split screen: a busy daycare lobby and a stressed director with a phone. Three text cards appear one at a time.
*"Here's what I see in most daycares. Three things.*
*One: empty seats. Not because families don't exist. Because an inquiry sits for hours, the tour doesn't convert, and nobody follows up.*
*Two: the team runs on the owner. Every question comes back to you. Who owns this task? What does a good result look like? Nobody wrote it down.*
*Three: you don't really know your numbers. Not just revenue, but what it costs you per child, what's left after payroll, and when the cash actually arrives.*
*More enrollment can create more pressure if the operation isn't ready for it. That's why I look at the actual bottleneck before anyone spends another dollar."*

### 5. WHY I'M TALKING TO DAYCARE OWNERS (3:20-4:10)
**VISUAL:** Aloha Capital and Aloha Growth logos [LOGOS]. A simple "$25M+ AUM" card. Daycare photo [PHOTO].
*"Since then I've taken the same approach into other businesses. I started Aloha Capital in 2023. It's real estate, and it now has over twenty-five million dollars in assets under management. I'm also a partner in a daycare, where I helped take it from zero to a six-figure business in less than a year. And I built Aloha Growth to help business owners improve their operations, sales and marketing. Different industries. Same question: are you building a business, or a job that needs you for everything?"*

### 6. THE METHOD (4:10-5:45)
**VISUAL:** Three-step graphic: Diagnose, Blueprint, Build. Each with an icon that animates in.
*"Here's how we work. Three steps.*
*Step one, Diagnose. We step inside your center. We look at your numbers: tuition per child, cost per child, payroll, margin, cash timing. We look at enrollment: how fast you respond, how many tours convert. We look at operations and people: your ratios, your scheduling, your turnover, what only you can do.*
*Step two, Blueprint. You get a written plan that names your number-one bottleneck and lays out the next twelve months, phase by phase, with owners and metrics.*
*Step three, Build. If you want, our team carries out that plan with you: the enrollment system, the tour and follow-up scripts, the procedures, the hiring and training, the reporting. And for centers ready to grow, we help plan the next location, including the real estate side through Aloha Capital."*

### 7. THE PACKAGES (5:45-6:50)
**VISUAL:** Four cards appear in sequence with durations.
*"There are four ways to start.*
*The Fill Sprint. Thirty days. We fix how you answer, tour and follow up, and we launch one campaign to fill your open seats.*
*The Growth Blueprint. Three weeks. A full diagnostic and a twelve-month plan.*
*Fill and Systemize. Ninety days. We build the enrollment engine, the procedures and the retention system with you.*
*And Scale Partner. Twelve months for centers ready to open or acquire their next location, with us as your operating partner."*

### 8. WHO THIS IS FOR (6:50-7:30)
**VISUAL:** Checklist ticking off. Then a short "Not for you if..." list in muted text.
*"This is for daycare owners in South Jersey and the Philadelphia area with at least five hundred thousand dollars in annual revenue, who are licensed and in good standing, who will open their books and let go of some decisions. It's not for you if you're looking for a quick trick, or you're not willing to change how the center runs."*

*"[IF REAL AND APPROVED: 'Here's what [NAME] said after working with us: ...'] [IF A GUARANTEE IS APPROVED: 'And here's how we back it: ...']"*

### 10. SCARCITY AND CTA (8:00-8:40)
**VISUAL:** "3" spot counter. Button graphic: "Apply for the Founding Cohort".
*"I'm opening a founding cohort of just three daycares. I want to get real results for these three and build the system around them. If that's you, click the button below this video and fill out the short application. If you qualify, you'll book a strategy call, and we'll look at your center together. The truck taught me that growth isn't about working harder. It's about building something that can run without you. Let's build that for your daycare."*

### 11. END CARD (8:40-8:50)
**VISUAL:** Logo, URL, button. Fade to black.

---

## Delivery notes for the avatar/voice
- Warm, direct, unhurried. Short sentences. Pause for one beat after each number.
- Smile on the lines about the truck and the daycare. Lower and slower on the problem section.
- Don't shout the CTA. Say it like an invitation.

## Compliance notes
- Do not say "guaranteed" or promise enrollment, revenue or profit outcomes unless counsel approves the wording.
- Do not present the daycare partnership as a result the viewer should expect.
- Add on-screen text during the numbers segment: "A&H Logistics, founder-reported revenue. Individual results vary."
