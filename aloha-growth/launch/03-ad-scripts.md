# Ad Scripts: Aloha Growth (Meta and YouTube)

**Audience:** daycare owners in South Jersey and Philadelphia. **Offer:** apply for the founding cohort or book a Fill Sprint. **Presenter:** the founder avatar.
Facts used are only those supplied by the founder. [BRACKETS] must be filled with real details or removed.

## Ad policy guardrails (Meta)
- Don't imply you know a viewer's personal traits ("Are you overwhelmed?"). Speak about the business: "Daycare owners..." works better than "You're struggling..."
- No before/after promises or guaranteed outcomes. No fake urgency beyond real cohort limits.
- Include a disclaimer where numbers appear: "Founder-reported. Results vary."
