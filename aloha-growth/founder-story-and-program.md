Three things I look at with every owner:
1. **Understand your numbers.** Cost to deliver, what's left after expenses, and when cash actually arrives. More sales can create more pressure if operations aren't ready.
2. **Make the work repeatable.** Who owns the task, what a good result looks like, how it's checked.
3. **Find the real bottleneck before spending more.** Leads, follow-up and delivery are different problems with different fixes.

## 2. The program: from six figures to seven to eight

### Who it's for
Daycare owners in South Jersey and the Philadelphia area already doing **at least $500K a year**, who are stuck being the bottleneck in their own center.

### Step 1: Diagnostic (we step inside the business)
We score four areas and name the single biggest bottleneck:
- **Numbers:** tuition per child, cost per child, payroll as a share of revenue, margin, when cash arrives.
- **Enrollment and marketing:** inquiry volume, tour-to-enrollment rate, waitlist, referral sources, reviews.
- **Operations:** occupancy by classroom, staff-to-child ratios, scheduling, turnover, parent communication.
- **People and systems:** documented processes, who owns each task, how the director's time is spent.

Output: a **Strengths and Weaknesses Report**.

### Step 2: Scale blueprint
A written, phased plan with owners, metrics and timelines:
- **Phase 1, Stabilize ($500K to $1M):** fix the numbers, fill open capacity, document the core work, get the owner out of daily decisions.
- **Phase 2, Systemize ($1M to $3M):** repeatable enrollment funnel, a trained director and lead-teacher layer, retention systems, a second classroom wing or location if the numbers support it.
- **Phase 3, Scale ($3M and up):** multi-location playbook, central back office, real estate strategy, financing.

### Step 3: Done-for-you implementation (optional)
If the owner wants it, our team carries out the blueprint: enrollment marketing, operations systems, hiring and training, and weekly reporting against the plan.

## 3. Founding cohort: 3 daycares

**Qualification criteria:**
- **At least $500K in annual revenue**
- Located in South Jersey or the Philadelphia area
- Licensed and in good standing
- The owner is willing to open the books and share numbers
- The owner will delegate decisions and follow the plan
- The bottleneck is fixable through enrollment, operations or systems

**Application questions:**
1. What is your annual revenue and current occupancy?
2. How many locations, classrooms and staff do you have?
3. Where do you feel stuck: enrollment, staffing, cash, or your own time?
4. What is your average tuition and your biggest expense?
5. What does success look like in 12 months?
6. Are you open to done-for-you implementation?

**Offer framing:** "Only 3 spots in the founding cohort. Full diagnostic and blueprint, with the option for us to build it with you."

## 4. How Aloha Growth makes money

These are options, not a final choice. Dollar figures are illustrative assumptions for a $750K daycare, so replace them with your real pricing.

| # | Model | How it works | Illustrative revenue per client | Pros | Cons |
|---|-------|--------------|---------------------------------|------|------|
| 1 | **Paid diagnostic and blueprint** | One-time fee for the audit and written plan | $5K-$15K | Cash upfront, low risk, filters serious owners | One-time, no ongoing income |
| 2 | **Monthly retainer (done-for-you)** | Flat monthly fee to implement the plan | $4K-$10K/month | Predictable recurring revenue | Owner may churn if results are slow |
| 3 | **Performance fee** | Percentage of the revenue or profit growth we create over a baseline | 10%-20% of incremental revenue or profit | Aligned incentives, big upside | Unpredictable, needs clean baseline tracking |
| 4 | **Equity or partnership** | Ownership stake in exchange for work, as in your existing daycare | 10%-30% of the business | Highest long-term upside, builds an asset portfolio | Capital tied up, slow payback, more risk |
| 5 | **Real estate tie-in via Aloha Capital** | Buy or lease the building and lease it to the daycare, or finance expansion sites | Rent plus appreciation | Turns operator growth into real estate deals, uses your $25M+ platform | Capital-heavy, needs careful underwriting and legal structure |
| 6 | **Back-office and vendor services** | Shared payroll, procurement, marketing or software for a fee or margin | 3%-8% of revenue | Recurring, scales across many clients | Requires building the service |

**Recommended starting mix:** a paid diagnostic (1) that converts into a retainer plus a small performance fee (2 + 3). Reserve equity (4) and the Aloha Capital real estate angle (5) for the best two or three clients once you've seen results. This keeps early cash flow healthy without tying up capital before the model is proven.

**Example, one $750K daycare, year one:** $10K diagnostic + $6K/month retainer x 12 ($72K) + 15% of $250K in added revenue ($37.5K) = about **$120K**. Three clients would be about **$360K**. These are assumptions, not projections.
