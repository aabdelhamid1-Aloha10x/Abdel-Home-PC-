# Aloha Growth: Founder Story and Scale Program

## 1. Founder story (built only from your Drive file)

In 2020, I rented one truck.

That truck became A&H Logistics, a transportation company that grew into an eight-figure business.

About fourteen years ago I moved from Egypt to the United States. I didn't come from money. I finished a bachelor's in finance, financial management and capital markets, and I started several ventures. Transportation became the biggest.

Today I'm also building Aloha Capital (real estate investing) and Aloha Growth, where we help business owners improve their operations, sales and marketing.

The lesson from going from one rented truck to eight figures: the hard part isn't the revenue number. It's the shift from running everything yourself to building a company that can grow without you. Are you building a business, or a job that needs you for everything?

Three things I look at with every owner:
1. **Understand your numbers.** Cost to deliver, what's left after expenses, and when cash actually arrives. More sales can create more pressure if operations aren't ready.
2. **Make the work repeatable.** Who owns the task, what a good result looks like, how it's checked.
3. **Find the real bottleneck before spending more.** Leads, follow-up and delivery are different problems with different fixes.

## 2. The Program: from six figures to seven to eight

**Who it's for:** owners doing roughly $100K-$1M+ a year who are stuck being the bottleneck.

### Step 1: Diagnostic (we step inside the business)
We look at four areas and score each one:
- **Numbers:** delivery cost, margin, cash-flow timing.
- **Sales and marketing:** lead flow, follow-up, close rate.
- **Operations and delivery:** capacity, quality, how much falls back on the owner.
- **People and systems:** documented processes, owners for each task, how work gets checked.

Output: a **Strengths and Weaknesses Report** naming the single biggest bottleneck.

### Step 2: Scale Blueprint
A written, phased plan with owners, metrics and a timeline:
- **Phase 1, Stabilize ($100K to $1M):** fix the numbers, document the core work, remove the owner from daily decisions.
- **Phase 2, Systemize ($1M to $5M):** repeatable sales process, delivery capacity, hiring and management structure.
- **Phase 3, Scale ($5M to 8 figures):** channel expansion, leadership layer, reporting cadence, capital and cash-flow strategy.

### Step 3: Done-for-you implementation (optional)
If they want it, our team carries out the blueprint: operations, sales and marketing, with weekly reporting against the plan.

## 3. Founding cohort: 3 businesses

**Qualification criteria (adjust to taste):**
- Already generating consistent six-figure revenue
- Profitable or near break-even, with real customer demand
- The owner is willing to open the books and share numbers
- The owner will hand over decisions and follow the plan
- The bottleneck is fixable through operations, sales or marketing (not a failing product)

**Application questions:**
1. What's your annual revenue and profit margin?
2. What do you do, and who do you serve?
3. How many people work in the business, and what falls only on you?
4. Where do you feel stuck: leads, follow-up, delivery, or cash?
5. What does success look like in 12 months?
6. Are you open to done-for-you implementation?

**Offer framing:** "Only 3 spots in the founding cohort. Full diagnostic and blueprint, with the option for us to build it with you."
