# Package 0: Fill Sprint (30 days)

Entry offer for daycares at $500K+ that have **open seats** but can't afford the full packages yet. It also works as the lead generator for the rest of the ladder. Prices and targets are illustrative assumptions.

## The offer
**Promise:** in 30 days we fix how your center answers, tours and follows up with families, and run one enrollment campaign to fill open seats.

**Price options (pick one per client):**
- **Flat:** $1,500-$3,000 one-time
- **Pay-from-results:** $2,500 upfront + 25%-40% of first-year tuition from newly enrolled children, capped at a set amount

**Fit criteria:**
- $500K+ annual revenue and licensed in good standing
- At least 5 open seats (or a waitlist problem in the other direction)
- Owner or director can commit 2-3 hours a week
- The problem is enrollment and follow-up, not location, licensing or owner conflict

**Guarantee (example):** if the center doesn't get at least **X qualified tours booked** in 30 days while following the plan, we run a second 30-day campaign at no charge. X is set from the baseline.

## Timeline
| When | Focus | What we do |
|------|-------|------------|
| **Days 1-3** | **Baseline** | Kickoff call. Record open seats by classroom, tuition rates, inquiries last 90 days, tour-to-enrollment rate, and current response time. Mystery-shop the center by phone and web form. |
| **Days 4-7** | **Quick fixes** | Rebuild Google Business profile (photos, hours, services, tuition range). Set up missed-call text-back and an inquiry auto-reply. Start a review-request text to current parents. |
| **Days 8-14** | **Tour and follow-up system** | Write the tour script and the 5-step follow-up sequence (text and email). Train the director and front desk on it in one role-play session. Build a simple inquiry tracker. |
| **Days 15-21** | **Campaign launch** | Launch one "free trial day" or "first-week" offer: a landing page, a local ad campaign (ad spend paid by the owner), and a flyer or email to the center's existing list and neighbors. |
| **Days 22-28** | **Optimize** | Review leads daily. Adjust ads and follow-up timing. Coach the director on tour calls and objections. |
| **Days 29-30** | **Results review** | Report on inquiries, tours, enrollments and open seats filled. Show what the seats are worth per year, then recommend the next step. |

## What's included
- Baseline and mystery shop
- Google profile rebuild, review requests, missed-call text-back
- Tour script and follow-up sequence
- One-session staff training
- One enrollment campaign build and management (ad spend separate)
- Weekly 30-minute call plus a short results report

## What's not included
- SOP library, hiring playbook, retention program (that's Package 2)
- Financial and unit-economics review (that's Package 1)
- Ad spend, licensing costs or software fees

## Metrics we track
Inquiries, response time, tours booked, tour-to-enrollment rate, seats filled, and annual tuition value of seats filled.

## Where it leads
- **Seats filled and owner wants more:** upgrade to Package 1 (Blueprint) or Package 2 (Fill and Systemize). Offer a credit of part of the Sprint fee if they sign within 30 days.
- **Not a fit:** the results report says why, so nobody wastes time.

## Risks to manage
- Pay-from-results only after the baseline confirms demand exists.
- Put fee terms, the definition of "newly enrolled" and the cap in a written agreement.
- Have counsel review any percentage-of-tuition arrangement.
