**Format:** 16:9, you on camera, seated or standing, eye level. Cut to B-roll and on-screen text where noted.
**Upload:** YouTube (unlisted), Vimeo or Loom. Paste the link into `siteConfig.explainerVideoUrl` in the Lovable project.

## Shooting notes

- **Setting:** a bright, warm room. Ideally in or near a daycare, or at your desk with a plant and soft daylight. Avoid a dark background; the page is navy, so a lighter shot stands out.
- **Audio:** a lavalier mic. Audio quality matters more than camera quality.
- **Delivery:** read it once off a teleprompter app, then do a second take talking it through naturally. The second take usually wins.
- **B-roll to grab while shooting:** you walking into a center, a whiteboard with the 5 steps, you reviewing a scorecard with someone, a "Now Enrolling" sign.
- **Captions:** add burned-in captions. Most people start the video muted. I can add those and the on-screen graphics in HyperFrames once you send the footage.
