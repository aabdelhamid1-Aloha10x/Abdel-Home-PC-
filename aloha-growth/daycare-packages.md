# Aloha Growth: Three Daycare Packages

For daycares in South Jersey and Philadelphia doing **$500K+ a year**. Each package is a step up from the one before, so an owner can start small and move up as results show. All prices, targets and percentages are **illustrative assumptions** to replace with your real numbers.

| | Package 1: Growth Blueprint | Package 2: Fill and Systemize | Package 3: Scale Partner |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Model** | Audit and plan | Done-with-you, 90 days, then monthly | Done-for-you plus equity or performance share |
| **Best for** | Owners who want proof before committing | Owners with empty seats and no system | Owners ready to grow to 2+ locations |
| **Length** | 3 weeks | 90 days, then optional monthly | 12 months, renewable |
| **Illustrative price** | $7,500 one-time | $15,000 upfront + $5,000/month (months 4+) | $8,000-$12,000/month + 10%-20% of incremental profit, or equity by agreement |
| **Cohort guarantee** | Blueprint credited toward Package 2 if they sign within 30 days | Enrollment target guarantee (see below) | Performance-based by design |

## Package 1: Growth Blueprint (3 weeks)

**Goal:** show the owner exactly where the business leaks money and what to fix first, in a plan they can use with or without us.

### Timeline
| When | What happens |
|------|--------------|
| **Week 0** | Kickoff call. Owner shares 12 months of financials, payroll, enrollment roster, tuition rates and the marketing they run today. |
| **Week 1** | We step inside: on-site visit, observe drop-off and pickup, tour the center, interview the director and 2-3 staff. Analyze the numbers: tuition per child, cost per child, payroll share, occupancy by classroom, margin, cash timing. |
| **Week 2** | Enrollment and marketing audit: inquiry sources, response time, tour-to-enrollment rate, waitlist, reviews, website and Google profile. Mystery-shop the center's inquiry process. Competitor scan of daycares within a 5-10 mile radius. |
| **Week 3** | Deliver the **Strengths and Weaknesses Report** and the **Scale Blueprint** in a 90-minute walkthrough with the owner. |

### What's included
- Financial and unit-economics review
- Enrollment funnel and marketing audit, including a mystery shop
- Staffing, ratio and scheduling review
- Written report naming the single biggest bottleneck
- 12-month phased blueprint with owners, metrics and timelines
- A recommended package and price for implementation, if they want it

### Success measure
The owner leaves with a prioritized plan and a number: "You have X open seats worth $Y a year."

## Package 2: Fill and Systemize (90 days)

**Goal:** fill empty capacity, fix the top bottleneck from the blueprint, and get the owner out of daily firefighting.

### Timeline
| When | Focus | Key deliverables |
|------|-------|------------------|
| **Days 1-14** | **Foundation** | Baseline metrics locked (occupancy, inquiries, tour-to-enrollment rate, payroll %). Start tracking weekly. Fix quick wins: Google Business profile, review requests, inquiry response within minutes, missed-call text-back. |
| **Days 15-30** | **Enrollment engine** | Launch the enrollment funnel: landing page, local ads, inquiry follow-up sequence (text and email), tour script, and enrollment-day checklist. Open house or "free trial day" offer built and scheduled. |
| **Days 31-60** | **Operations and people** | Document the core SOPs (opening and closing, ratios, parent communication, incident handling). Director and lead-teacher role scorecards. Hiring and onboarding playbook. Staff scheduling fix to cut overtime and turnover. |
| **Days 61-90** | **Retention and referrals** | Parent referral program, milestone communications, tuition-collection and late-fee process, waitlist management. Owner's weekly dashboard. 90-day results review and next-step plan. |

### What's included
- Everything in Package 1 (blueprint included)
- Enrollment funnel build and ad management for the first 90 days (ad spend paid by the owner)
- Tour and inquiry scripts, plus role-play training for front-desk staff
- SOP library and role scorecards
- Weekly 60-minute call plus a shared dashboard
- Direct messaging access for questions

### Guarantee (example)
If the center doesn't add **enrollments equal to at least X% of open capacity** within 90 days while following the plan, we keep working at no charge until it does. X is set from the blueprint.

### After day 90
Owner moves to a **$5,000/month** ongoing plan (weekly call, dashboard, ad management) or graduates to Package 3.

## Package 3: Scale Partner (12 months)

**Goal:** turn a strong single center into a multi-location business, with us carrying most of the execution and sharing in the upside.

### Timeline
| When | Focus | Key deliverables |
|------|-------|------------------|
| **Months 1-3** | **Fix and fill** | Same as Package 2's 90 days, run by our team. Baseline, funnel, SOPs, retention. Target: 90%+ occupancy at the flagship center. |
| **Months 4-6** | **Profit and leadership** | Cost-per-child optimization, tuition and pricing review, subsidy and payment-mix review. Hire and train a director so the owner steps back. Monthly financial review with the owner. |
| **Months 7-9** | **Expansion planning** | Site selection and demand analysis for a second location in the target market. Financing options and lease or purchase terms, with an Aloha Capital review where the owner opts in. Licensing timeline mapped with the owner's counsel. |
| **Months 10-12** | **Launch and repeat** | Pre-open enrollment campaign for the second location. Hiring wave and training using the SOP library. Central back-office setup (payroll, procurement, reporting). 12-month review and multi-location roadmap. |

### What's included
- Everything in Package 2, executed by our team
- Weekly leadership call plus a monthly financial review
- Director and management hiring support
- Expansion planning: market analysis, financing options, opening plan
- Back-office setup for multiple locations
- Optional Aloha Capital real estate review for buying or leasing sites

### Compensation options (pick one per client)
- **Retainer plus performance:** $8,000-$12,000/month plus 10%-20% of incremental profit above the baseline agreed in the blueprint.
- **Retainer plus equity:** a reduced retainer plus a **minority stake** in the daycare or the new location, negotiated case by case and papered by counsel. This mirrors how you took part in your existing daycare.

### Guardrails
- Limit this to **1-2 clients at a time**, since it needs the most of your team's time and possibly capital.
- Take equity only in clients with strong books, a good owner relationship, and a clean license record.

## How the three fit together
1. **Package 1** is the front door and the main sales tool. It's low-risk for the owner and pays for itself in cash and insight.
2. **Package 2** is the core recurring product and where most clients should land.
3. **Package 3** is for the best two or three clients and is where the largest long-term upside sits.

**Founding cohort of 3:** offer the first three qualifying daycares a founding-cohort rate on Package 2, and use their results as case studies.

## Assumptions to confirm before using
- Prices and percentages above are placeholders, not benchmarks.
- The enrollment guarantee needs a baseline and a clear definition before it is offered.
- NJ and PA licensing, ratio and subsidy rules affect staffing and expansion plans. Have a local child-care compliance advisor confirm those details for each client.
- Ad spend, licensing fees and any real estate costs are paid by the owner, separate from our fees.
