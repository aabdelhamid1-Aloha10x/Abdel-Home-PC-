## 4. Pricing benchmarks by segment
- Solo / 2-5 trucks: $20-$40/mo flat (TruckingOffice, Rigbooks, TruckLogics owner-op $39.95); $99-$199/user (Tailwind Pro); $99/mo AI-dispatch bundle (Numeo Starter); Alvys ~$3/load or $150+ (unverified). Willingness to pay for core TMS tops out near $50-$150/mo.
- 6-50 trucks: TruckLogics $80-$250/mo flat; Truckbase ~$290+ (third party); Numeo Growth $499/mo; Tailwind $99-$199/user; Fleetio $4-$10/vehicle add-on; ELD $25-$50/vehicle. A 20-truck fleet pays roughly $300-$800/mo for TMS.
- 50+ trucks: Rose Rocket ~$2,080+/mo flat; Revenova $2,500+/mo; McLeod/Trimble/Descartes quote only plus implementation; Numeo top ~$1,999 (200 trucks). Implies ~$15-$40/truck/mo effective.
- Dispatch companies: seat-priced ($99-$199/user Tailwind; $49 per extra AI seat Numeo; ~$65 Axon guess; Alvys $399-$500/user claims). Real value metric is trucks or loads managed (they take 5-10% of gross, bg); per-seat pricing is the main pain point, so a hybrid base + per-managed-truck is open ground.

## 5. Recommended tiers and price points (hypotheses, to validate)
Value metric: active trucks (billed monthly, annual -15%), with unlimited drivers/users in all tiers; metered AI on top. Reason: avoids Tailwind/Alvys seat friction and matches how fleets think.

| Tier | Target | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | 1-5 trucks | $39/mo flat (up to 3 trucks), +$9/truck after | Loads, dispatch board, driver PWA, rate-con and invoice PDF, DVIR, tracking links, QuickBooks, 123Loadboard link |
| Fleet | 6-50 trucks | $14/truck/mo, $149 minimum | Starter plus settlements, factoring recon, fuel import, IFTA, maintenance, compliance/DQ, chat, ELD integration, load boards |
| Enterprise | 50+ | $9-$12/truck/mo volume, $1,500 min, quote | Plus EDI, API, SSO, customer portal, custom reports, SLA, onboarding fee $2,500-$7,500 |
| Dispatch Co | Dispatcher selling services | $149/mo base + $6/managed truck/mo (owner-ops as sub-tenants, per-carrier settlement and statements) + 3 seats included, $19 extra | Multi-carrier workspace, dispatcher commission/fee statements, carrier onboarding packets |

Add-ons:
- AI Dispatcher (metered): bundle credits. Benchmark Numeo $49/seat, $99-$499 tier. Suggest $79/mo with 200 AI actions (load search, broker calls, rate negotiation), then ~$0.40/action or $1-$2 per brokered-call minute; cap by truck count. Value story: one human dispatcher costs far more.
- Recruiting/onboarding: Tenstreet $300-$2,500/mo benchmark, HireRight $40-$80 per check pass-through. Suggest $99/mo base (funnel, SMS) + $0.05 per SMS and $0.25-$0.50 per AI-call minute (metered) + pass-through screening at cost +15%, or per-hire $25-$49. Undercuts Tenstreet at small-carrier scale.
- ELD/telematics: resell/integrate (Motive, Samsara) rather than build; $0 integration fee.
- Premium: Maintenance Pro (Fleetio $4-$10/vehicle benchmark) at +$3/truck; Compliance/DQ +$2/truck; SMS pack; extra storage.
- Setup: free for Starter/Fleet (competitor norm: Tailwind none); paid onboarding for Enterprise and data migration $500-$2,500.

## 6. Differentiators for an automation-first TMS
1. Recruiting funnel with SMS + AI voice built in (nobody in the TMS set has it; Tenstreet is separate and expensive).
2. Dispatch-company multi-tenancy: per-carrier books, auto statements, sub-tenant owner-op portals; competitors are seat-priced.
3. AI dispatcher inside the TMS (load search, broker calls, reconcile rates) vs Numeo standalone and unintegrated.
4. DVIR/maintenance/compliance on one system that triggers dispatch holds (out-of-service, expired DQ).
5. Factoring submit + reconciliation with auto-matching; settlements that are correct and explainable.
6. Driver PWA no-install, SMS onboarding; low-touch self-serve setup (Rose Rocket wins on fast setup, Alvys on ease; McLeod loses).
7. Transparent published pricing (most competitors hide it: Alvys, Truckbase, McLeod, Motive).

## 7. Table-stakes (must have for credibility)
- Solo/2-5: rate-con generation + invoice PDF, load board link-out, IFTA reports, expense/driver pay basics, QuickBooks export, doc scan/upload, low flat price.
- 6-50: settlements that are correct, factoring integration, ELD integration, tracking links/customer notifications, load board posting, fuel cards import, maintenance/DVIR, compliance/DQ, driver app, QuickBooks sync.
- 50+: EDI (204/990/214/210), API/webhooks, role-based access, audit trails, accounting GL integration, reporting/BI, SSO, SLA/support.
- Dispatch company: multi-carrier tenant isolation, carrier onboarding packets/agreements (W9, COI, authority check), dispatcher fee/commission statements, owner-op settlements, per-carrier branding/portal, carrier packet e-sign.

McLeod: steep learning curve, long onboarding, add-on modules. Trimble: premium price, paid support/professional services. Motive: auto-renewal, difficult downgrade, hardware lock-in. Rose Rocket: priced for larger operators. Alvys: opaque pricing. Truckbase/Alvys/Rose Rocket reviews are small-sample (15-17 reviews on G2).
