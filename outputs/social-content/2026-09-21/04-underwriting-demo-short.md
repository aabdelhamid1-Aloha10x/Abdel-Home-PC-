## Teleprompter script

> Before you say yes to any rental deal, do this math. Takes 60 seconds.
>
> Say the deal is asking $4,200 a month in rent.
>
> First: subtract real expenses. Taxes, insurance, maintenance reserve, vacancy — not just the mortgage. Say that's $1,400.
>
> That leaves $2,800 a month in net operating income. $33,600 a year.
>
> Now divide that by the purchase price. If it's listed at $480,000, that's a 7% cap rate.
>
> Compare that number to what similar properties in the area are actually trading at. If comparable deals are trading at a 5, this one's priced better than the market — worth a closer look. If they're trading at 9, this one's overpriced for what it delivers.
>
> One ratio. Sixty seconds. Save this and run it on the next deal someone sends you.
