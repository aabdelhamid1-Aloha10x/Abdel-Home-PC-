1. **(Recommended)** "There's a wall almost every business hits — and it has nothing to do with how much money you're making." *(Contrast/inversion — sets up the reveal that it's an operating problem, not a revenue problem.)*
2. "I rented one truck in 2020. Here's the exact moment that stopped being the hard part." *(Personal, but needs Abdel input #1 above to be specific — falls back to generic if unanswered.)*

## Teleprompter script (generic version — safe to record today)

> There's a wall almost every business hits. It has nothing to do with how much money you're making.
>
> It's the moment you realize the business can't grow past what you, personally, can do in a day.
>
> Answering every call. Approving every decision. Being the only one who knows how something actually works.
>
> That's not a revenue problem. That's a systems problem.
>
> The fix isn't "work harder." It's building three things before you need them: a written process for the job you keep doing yourself, someone trained to own it, and a way to check the work without redoing it.
>
> I built a transportation company from one truck. The thing that let it scale wasn't hustle — it was getting out of my own way, one task at a time.
>
> Comment "SYSTEMS" and I'll send you the framework I use to figure out what to hand off first.
