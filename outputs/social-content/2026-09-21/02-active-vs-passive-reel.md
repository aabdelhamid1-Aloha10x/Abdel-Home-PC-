# Concept 2 — Real Estate Education Reel
## "Active vs. Passive Real Estate: The 4 Questions That Actually Decide It"

**Content ID**: 2026-09-21-R2 · **Pillar**: Real estate education · **Audience**: US professionals/business owners considering real estate exposure, deciding whether to operate a property themselves or back someone who does
**Objective**: Discovery + trust — positions Abdel as someone who thinks about both sides honestly, not someone pitching a fund
**Length**: 40s · **Format**: Vertical 9:16, talking head, comparison/split framing
**Trend basis**: direct-comparison hook pattern (`@homeloanswithluis`, 44.1x — "stocks or real estate") + hidden-flaw/due-diligence framing (`@ron_apke`, 53.6x) — see `RESEARCH.md`. Proposed adaptation.
**Compliance note**: this script deliberately contains **no Aloha Capital-specific figures or offer terms** — it's a general decision framework, so it does not require the compliance clearance that gates the existing `aloha-capital-content/video-concepts/` scripts. Do not add fund-specific numbers to this script without running it through that same guardrail.
**Primary CTA**: "Comment 'ACTIVE' or 'PASSIVE' — tell me which one you are."

## Three hook options

1. **(Recommended)** "Everyone asks me if they should buy real estate. Wrong question." *(Contrarian — reframes before answering.)*
2. "Active or passive real estate — most people pick based on excitement, not on what they actually have time for." *(Direct, sets up the comparison immediately.)*
3. "You don't need to become a landlord to own real estate. You need to answer four questions first." *(Leads with the payoff — lower curiosity gap, but very clear value prop.)*

## Teleprompter script

> Everyone asks me if they should buy real estate. Wrong question.
>
> The real question is: active, or passive. Do you want to operate a property, or do you want real estate exposure without operating anything.
>
> Four questions decide it.
>
> One — do you have the time to find, underwrite, and manage a deal yourself, or do you have the money to pay someone else to?
>
> Two — can you personally absorb a bad tenant, a bad contractor, or a slow market? Or do you need someone spreading that risk across multiple projects?
>
> Three — do you want to learn the mechanics, or do you want the outcome?
>
> Four — how fast do you need your money back? Active deals can move faster. Passive positions usually lock up longer.
>
> There's no wrong answer. There's only the wrong answer for you.
>
> Comment ACTIVE or PASSIVE — tell me which one you are, and I'll tell you what to look at next.
