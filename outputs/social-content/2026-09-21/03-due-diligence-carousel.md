## Slide-by-slide

**Slide 1 — Cover**
- Copy: "5 THINGS DUE DILIGENCE ACTUALLY CATCHES" / subhead: "(that the listing never tells you)"
- Visual: Full-canvas photo of Abdel, arms crossed, confident/serious expression — direct eye contact with camera. Warm neutral background matching the account's existing aesthetic.

**Slide 2 — The setup**
- Copy: "Two properties can look identical on paper. One is a good deal. One is a trap. The difference is never in the listing photos."

- Takeaway: frames why this list matters before listing the 5 items.

**Slide 3 — Item 1**
- Copy: "1. TITLE & ACCESS — Does the property have clear, legal, recorded access? A landlocked or easement-dependent parcel can be worthless no matter how good the price looks."
- Visual: No presenter image — full-canvas simple graphic (two parcel outlines, one with a clear road connection, one without) matching the "hidden flaw" reference pattern.

**Slide 4 — Item 2**
- Copy: "2. REAL EXPENSES — The listing shows gross rent. It doesn't show taxes, insurance, deferred maintenance, or vacancy. Get the real trailing 12 months, not the pro forma."
- Visual: Abdel pointing at camera, direct/instructive expression.

**Slide 5 — Item 3**
- Copy: "3. TENANT QUALITY — A lease at a high rent means nothing if the tenant can't actually pay it. Ask for payment history, not just the lease terms."
- Visual: No presenter — simple document/checkmark graphic, full canvas.

**Slide 6 — Item 4**
- Copy: "4. ZONING & USE — What's allowed on this property today may not be what you're assuming. Confirm current zoning matches your plan before you're under contract to close, not after."
- Visual: Abdel, slightly furrowed brow, cautionary expression.

**Slide 7 — Item 5 + CTA**
- Copy: "5. EXIT LIQUIDITY — Can you actually sell or refinance this when you need to, or are you the only buyer this asset type has? / Due diligence isn't paperwork. It's the difference between a deal and a mistake. / Comment 'CHECKLIST' and I'll send you the full version."
- Visual: Abdel, confident/resolved expression, direct eye contact — mirrors slide 1's pose to bookend the carousel.

## Visual direction & information hierarchy

- One full-canvas composition per slide — no nested frames, no small footer paragraphs, consistent with the account's need to stay readable on a phone.
- Presenter slides (1, 2, 4, 6, 7) alternate with graphic-only slides (3, 5) to keep visual rhythm and avoid five nearly-identical talking-head photos in a row.
- Typography: bold condensed sans for headlines (matches the visual identity direction already established in `aloha-capital-content/brand-brief.md`), consistent placement (upper or lower third) across all slides so the swipe feels like one system.
- Expression/pose per brief section 9: slide 1 confident (opening claim), slide 2 concerned (the trap being possible), slides 4/6 instructive/cautionary (teaching), slide 7 confident/resolved (closing, bookends slide 1).

A reusable 5-point checklist readers can mentally run on any deal before signing — not tied to one property or one asset class.
