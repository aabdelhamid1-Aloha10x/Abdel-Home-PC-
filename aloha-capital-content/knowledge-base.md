## Company

Aloha Capital — a real estate private lending and investment firm (private
lending brand: **Aloha Private Lending**, `alohaprivatelending.com`).
Boulder, CO based. Related/sibling platform: **Swell** (passive note
investment platform, `swell.investments`).

## Program 1 — Aloha LTD Income Fund (the flagship fund)

- **What it is**: an evergreen fund giving passive investors diversified
  exposure to a professionally managed portfolio of high-yield, **first
  position** loans on single-family and multi-family residential
  properties, across **50+ markets**.
- **Track record cited**: 5-year track record, "60+ consecutive months of
  positive returns," average annual net return cited as **10%+** in some
  sources, **8–9% target** in others (an unlevered-fund page). **Reconcile
  this before quoting a number on camera.**
- **Minimum investment**: cited as **$50,000** in most sources, **$100,000**
  in one (a "High Yield Income Fund" variant page) — confirm which minimum
  applies to which product/date before using it in a script.
- **Liquidity**: quarterly liquidity, no lock-up per most sources; one
  source specifies **45 days' notice** for quarterly redemptions.
- **Risk posture (as marketed)**: first-lien position on every loan, loans
  issued to "seasoned real estate investors" who personally guarantee the
  note; over **4,000 loans issued**, **0.40% default rate** cited.

## Program 2 — Aloha Passive Note Platform

- **What it is**: instead of buying into the pooled fund, investors pick
  individual short-term notes, each collateralized by residential real
  estate and personally guaranteed by the borrowing real estate investor.
- **Returns cited**: fixed returns in the **10–13%** range in most sources;
  one source says **9–14%** for hand-picked notes.
- **Minimum investment**: cited as **$25,000** per note.
- **Positioning difference vs. the Fund**: more control (you choose the
  specific note/deal), less diversification than the pooled fund; fixed
  term instead of evergreen/quarterly-liquidity structure.

## Program 3 — Direct lending to investors (not the audience for this content)

Aloha Capital also originates the loans themselves: fast bridge and
construction financing for **fix & flip**, **fix & hold (BRRRR)**, and
**multifamily value-add / infill development** deals. This is the
borrower-side business, not an investment product — useful for credibility
("we're not a fund manager sitting on the sidelines, we originate and
underwrite the loans ourselves"), but the content plan below is aimed at
capital, i.e. the investor side (Programs 1 and 2).

## Positioning hook this unlocks

Aloha Capital can credibly claim something most "real estate investing"
content on Reels/TikTok can't: **you don't have to become a landlord,
flipper, or wholesaler to get real-estate-secured returns.** Nearly every
outlier video found in `trend-research.md` is teaching people how to *do*
real estate deals themselves. Aloha's actual product is the answer for the
audience that watches those videos and doesn't want to do any of that
personally — first-lien-secured, professionally underwritten, passive.
That contrast is the core content wedge (see `brand-brief.md`).
