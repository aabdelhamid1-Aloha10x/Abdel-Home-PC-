# Trend Research — Real Estate Investing Short-Form (vidIQ, live pull Sept 2026)

Source: vidIQ Instagram/TikTok outlier search, query "real estate investing
fund passive income returns explainer," audience-rescored for US adults
30–60 interested in passive real estate income / debt-fund investing.
Outlier score = multiple of the creator's own median views, so these are
posts that *overperformed the creator's own baseline*, not just big
accounts.

## Pattern extraction — what's actually working right now

1. **Numbers as the hook.** Every top performer leads with a specific
   number in the first 3 seconds: "$320,000," "20 Ways," "30 Years of
   Depreciation in ONE Year," "$3,000 A Month Guaranteed," "$100,000."
   → Aloha's hooks should lead with real, confirmed project numbers —
   "$550,000 → $2,363,077," "$1.8M bought, sold for $3M" — not abstract
   claims. (An earlier version of this note cited a fixed 10–13%/0.40%
   default-rate figure sourced from the wrong company — see the revision
   history in `knowledge-base.md`. Use only the confirmed `list_projects`
   figures for hooks until the note-tranche's real numbers are confirmed
   against the PPM.)
2. **Contrarian/myth framing outperforms straight education.** "The
   ugliest house on the block," "the worst type of land to buy... or is
   it," "you might not always [have the income lenders want]" — these all
   invert an assumption in the hook. → Aloha's myth-vs-mechanism pillar
   (see `brand-brief.md`) should invert "you need to flip/landlord to make
   real-estate money."
3. **Talking-head + b-roll intercut, fast pacing, dynamic captions is the
   dominant format** across both platforms — not polished ads, not pure
   voiceover-over-b-roll. Matches well with a HeyGen avatar cut against
   property/document b-roll.
4. **POV / "expected vs. actual" case-study format** (the sober-living
   creator, the multifamily-tour creator) drives some of the single
   highest outlier multiples (36–1400x median) by making the number
   concrete and personal rather than abstract. → Aloha's proof/
   transparency pillar should show an actual dollar amount and its actual
   payout, not just the program's headline rate.
5. **Effort bar is low on the best performers.** Most are "within a day"
   or "within an hour" productions — a single location, a phone, direct
   address. This is achievable with a HeyGen avatar shoot plus stock/
   licensed b-roll; no need for a large physical production.
6. **Audio is simple**: voice-only or voice + light background music
   dominates; no complex sound design needed. Music choice matters more
   for pacing/energy than for production value.

## Round 2 — commercial value-add / before-after transformation (pulled after confirming Aloha10x's actual business)

Same method, query retargeted to "commercial real estate value-add
redevelopment before and after transformation retail industrial,"
audience-rescored for accredited/HNW investors interested in CRE private
equity and value-add development.

7. **Before/after transformation is the single highest-performing pattern
   found across both research passes.** A rusted grain silo → luxury home
   reel hit **1.7M views, 136.1x** the creator's median; a 35-acre raw
   land → family compound drone timelapse hit **1.5M views, 180.4x**; a
   run-down bungalow renovation hit **366.6K, 82.3x**. This is a much
   bigger effect size than the general education/myth patterns above.
   → This should be the **primary visual structure** for Aloha's content,
   not a secondary motif — see the "before/after split or reveal" motif in
   `brand-brief.md`.
8. **Off-market/exclusive-access framing performs even on a single
   creator's account**: a "need a buyer/partner, off-market opportunity,
   DM me" post hit 1.7M views, 337.4x median — scarcity + insider access
   is a strong secondary hook layer on top of the transformation.
9. **Drone establishing shots recur as the opening frame** across both
   silo and land-compound examples — matches the property b-roll direction
   already planned for HeyGen/Higgsfield generation.
