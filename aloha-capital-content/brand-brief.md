# Aloha Capital — Brand Brief for Short-Form Video

## Positioning statement

> Aloha Capital is where your capital goes to work in real estate — without
> you having to become a landlord, flipper, or wholesaler. First-lien
> security, professional underwriting, a program for your minimum and your
> risk appetite.

The wedge (see `knowledge-base.md`): the entire real-estate-investing
content category on social is "how to do a deal yourself." Aloha's audience
is the person watching that content who wants the *outcome* — real-estate-
secured returns — without taking on the *work*. Every video should
implicitly or explicitly make that contrast.

## Audience

Primarily the person the trend research turned up watching real-estate
Reels/TikToks: 28–55, has investable cash (post-401k-max, post-emergency-
fund, or an exit/liquidity event), values passive income, is either
skeptical of the stock market's real-estate-adjacent options (REITs) or
just wants diversification, and doesn't have time or interest to source,
underwrite, and manage deals personally. Two sub-segments map directly to
the two programs:

- **"I want it simple and diversified"** → Aloha LTD Income Fund ($50K+,
  quarterly liquidity, pooled/professionally managed).
- **"I want to pick my own deals"** → Passive Note Platform ($25K+ per
  note, fixed term, hand-selected).

## Voice

- Confident, plain-spoken, numbers-forward — not hypey. The credibility
  comes from specificity (first-lien position, default rate, track record),
  not superlatives.
- Never "get rich quick." Never "guaranteed." Every performance claim gets
  a soft landing ("targeted," "historically," "past performance doesn't
  guarantee future results") — see the compliance note in
  `knowledge-base.md`.
- Educational first, pitch second. Match the format the trend research
  shows winning: teach something real (how first-lien position protects
  you, how quarterly liquidity works, what "note investing" actually
  means), then point at Aloha as the vehicle.

## Visual identity direction (for Premiere/Higgsfield)

- **Palette**: warm neutral base (sand, warm white) + a single confident
  accent — deep ocean teal or a rich green (ties to "aloha"/growth without
  being a cliché tropical palette). Avoid the generic finance-bro palette
  (harsh black/gold/red).
- **Typography**: bold condensed sans for on-screen captions/numbers (the
  trend research shows every winning video uses dynamic caption overlays
  and big number callouts — "10–13%", "$25K min", "0.40% default rate").
  A cleaner serif or humanist sans for the logo/brand lockup only.
- **B-roll motifs**: property exteriors and drone establishing shots (per
  the outlier pattern), whiteboard/underwriting-math inserts, document/
  paperwork close-ups (deed, note, wire confirmation) to visualize "your
  money, secured by a real asset" — not stock-photo skyline shots.
- **Avatar (HeyGen)**: Abdel's avatar as the on-camera presenter/narrator —
  matches the highest-performing format found (talking-head + b-roll
  intercuts, fast pacing, dynamic captions).

## Content pillars

1. **Explainer** — how the specific program works, in plain terms (first
   video concept).
2. **Myth-vs-mechanism** — take a real-estate-investing myth or common
   Reels trope ("you have to flip houses to make real estate money") and
   show the passive alternative (second video concept).
3. **Proof/transparency** — walk through real numbers: what a specific
   investment amount actually pays out, expected-vs-actual style, borrowed
   from the highest-performing "case study" pattern in the trend research
   (third video concept).
4. *(Future, once more data/assets exist)* Borrower-side credibility clips
   (a funded deal, a rehabbed property) — social proof that Aloha
   originates and underwrites, doesn't just manage other people's paper.

## Compliance guardrail (repeat from knowledge-base.md — do not skip)

Any script with a specific return, minimum, or track-record number is
**draft-for-review**, not publish-ready, until Aloha Capital's
compliance/legal contact confirms current figures and required disclosures.
Treat this brief and the concepts in `video-concepts/` as creative +
structural direction, not final copy.
