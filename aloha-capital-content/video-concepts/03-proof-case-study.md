## Production notes

- **Highest compliance sensitivity of the three concepts** — it cites an
  actual realized payout, which is a step beyond a general program
  description. Get explicit sign-off on the exact figures and required
  disclosure language before this one is recorded or published, even in
  draft/internal review cuts.
- If a real investor's numbers aren't available/approved for use yet,
  either substitute a clearly-labeled hypothetical ("Here's a hypothetical
  example...") or hold this concept until real, approved data exists —
  don't quietly soften the specificity, since specificity is exactly what
  makes this format outperform (per the outlier data).
- Redact any account numbers/personal identifiers in the document insert
  b-roll.
