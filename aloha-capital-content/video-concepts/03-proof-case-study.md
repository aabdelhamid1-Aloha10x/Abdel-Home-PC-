# Concept 3 — Proof/Realized Case Study: "We Bought an Office Building for $1.8M. Here's What It Actually Sold For."

**Pillar**: Proof / realized case study · **Length**: 40s · **Format**:
Vertical 9:16, before/after + expected-vs-actual, avatar + document inserts
**Trend basis**: expected-vs-actual case study (`trend-research.md`
pattern 4) + before/after transformation (pattern 7) — this is the only
concept of the three built entirely on a **closed, realized** deal, which
makes it the lowest compliance risk and the strongest proof point.
**Anchor project**: 3 Waterside Crossing, Windsor, CT — office
repositioning. **Status: Sold.** Purchase $1,800,000 → sold $3,000,000.
Realized profit $1,000,000. Total return 55.56% over the hold — an
**actual outcome**, not a projection.
**Primary CTA**: "This is a closed deal — link in bio for what's open now."

## Production notes

- **This is the safest concept to produce and publish first** — every
  number is a closed transaction, not a forecast, which sidesteps most of
  the "projected vs. realized" compliance risk in Concepts 1 and 2. Still
  needs a compliance pass (disclosure language, whether this counts as a
  "testimonial"/performance advertisement under applicable rules), but
  there's no forecasting-language risk to manage.
- Scene 4's montage must not imply the other projects have already
  achieved their projected numbers — label each honestly by its real
  `list_projects` status (Stabilized / Acquired), only 3 Waterside
  Crossing gets "Sold."
- Redact any deal-party-identifying details in the closing-statement
  document insert beyond what's already public/appropriate to share.
