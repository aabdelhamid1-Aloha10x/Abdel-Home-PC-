## Production notes

- Scene 1's "DIY" b-roll should stay generic/stock — do not use a
  competitor's or another creator's actual footage/likeness.
- Scene 2's cut is the whole joke/hook of the video (small residential →
  large commercial) — the jump in dollar figures needs to land hard and
  fast; keep it under 1 second between the two numbers appearing.
- Comment-to-DM CTA depends on Aloha having a DM automation set up for the
  keyword — confirm this exists before publishing, same note as the prior
  draft of this concept.
