## Production notes

- This one leans on the diagram in scene 3 — build it as a clean animated
  graphic (Higgsfield motion graphics or a Premiere-built lower-third), not
  a photo. It's the single idea the whole video pivots on, so it needs to
  be instantly legible at a glance, sound off.
- Comment-to-DM CTA ("comment NOTES") mirrors the engagement-bait pattern
  seen across the outlier set (e.g. @mikeandbo's "Comment 'INFO'") — pair
  with whatever DM automation/ManyChat-equivalent Aloha already runs, if
  any; flag to the team if none exists yet, since the CTA depends on it.
