## Creative patterns (Polsia AI page)
- Mass variant testing: ~50 ads titled "Watch an AI Build Companies in Real Time" created within minutes on ~Oct 21-22 2026 (e.g. https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1128703499527884, https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=4070702233224168). Another batch of ~17 created Oct 22 with no link title (e.g. https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1114634851265641).
- Format mix (Sprites, all 499 Meta ads): 81% video, 11% static, 8% carousel. Landing: polsia.com home. CTA mostly "Learn More".
- Longest-running ad (135 days): "This AI runs everything: product, marketing, operations… while you do nothing."
- Creator partnership ads: 108 ads across 25 creator/publisher pages (Polsia Milcah, aiwithcharls, jakcreateswithpolsia, benkimball.ai, Sean In Business, MarketerHub, Thatgirlstudent Study hub, several "hustle.<name>" accounts). Reach shown on samples: 4K-38K per ad.
- A review reports Polsia recruiting UGC creators on Reddit (~$700/month retainer). Unverified.

## Google (Sprites)
- ~99 ads: 47 image, 27 video, 25 text. Markets: US, CA, GB, BE, JP, NL, IL, KR. Impression bands mostly <1K, one 45-50K.
- Copy: "Polsia — AI That Runs Your Company While You Sleep", "Build Your Business Overnight Now", "Free Trial Available - AI Startups: Grow Faster", "Custom-Built AI System - Free Trial Available".

## Target audience (inferred)
Side-hustlers and aspiring entrepreneurs, students, AI-tool enthusiasts, creators/marketers; English plus Spanish-speaking "hustle" audiences. Angle: passive income, "while you sleep", "do nothing".

## Spend
Not disclosed for US commercial ads. Sprites shows a modeled monthly range behind a paywall. Ad count jumped sharply after the $30M raise (May 2026); the founder earlier said he barely ran ads.

## Lessons for Unstaffd
1. Copy: creator partnership ads at scale + daily batch testing of 20-50 variants.
2. Avoid: "do nothing" hype. Polsia's founder named expectation mismatch as the main churn driver; it's also an FTC risk. Our angle: honest autopilot + a coach.
3. Improve on their Mode 1: Polsia runs user-company ads under the "Polsia" page name. Unstaffd should run them from the **client's own Page/brand** (ad account on our business portfolio, Page owned by the client), so customers see the real business.
