## 2. Launch funnel

### Pre-launch (6-8 weeks)
- **Waitlist page**: one input box ("type your idea"). Visitors get a free validation score immediately, then join the waitlist to get it built. It shows the product in 60 seconds.
- **Referral queue**: invite friends to move up the list.
- **Founding member offer**: first 500 get bonus credits for life, a founder badge, and entry into contest season 1.
- **Daily build in public** on every platform (see master spec §17), plus the AI CEO account.
- **Masar live**: weekly "AI is finishing Masar" episodes.
- **Private beta**: 50-100 users; collect real results and testimonials (real ones only).

### Launch week
- Product Hunt (Tue-Thu) + Show HN + launch thread on X/LinkedIn + YouTube premiere ("I let AI run a company for 30 days") + Reddit + newsletters, all the same day.
- Affiliates and creators post on launch day (deals signed in pre-launch).
- **Live stream**: "Give us an idea, the AI builds the business in 20 minutes", using viewers' ideas.
- Every launch-day signup gets a live preview site → people share their own site.

### Post-launch
- Weekly public metrics thread; monthly niche campaign; case studies; contest leaderboard updates.

## 3. Built-in viral loops

1. **"Built & run by Crewzero" badge** on every customer site (required on low tiers, removable on higher ones). Every customer site advertises us.
2. **Shareable idea score card**: "My AI CEO rated my idea 82/100" with a nice image for X/IG/LinkedIn. Free to generate, very shareable.
3. **Referral credits** on both sides.
4. **Public leaderboard + Founder Grant Contest**, with winners announced publicly.
5. **Public "companies launched today" ticker** and live revenue counter (real numbers).
6. **Public challenges**: "$0 to first sale in 14 days", with participants posting their progress.
7. **Agent clips**: short videos of agents working, posted by the AI CEO account daily.

## 4. Fundraising through build in public

### Options
| Path | When it makes sense |
|---|---|
| Stay bootstrapped | Solo + AI keeps burn low (~$1-2K/mo fixed). Raise only if growth needs compute/ad money |
| Accelerators | YC (accepts solo founders), a16z speedrun, Neo, South Park Commons: good once the product is live and growing |
| Angels / pre-seed SAFE | After ~$10-30K MRR with fast week-over-week growth, or exceptional engagement data |
| Community round (Wefunder / Republic) | Fits build in public: users and followers become investors and promoters |

### What investors will look at (publish these from day one)
- Recurring MRR growth (kept separate from one-off revenue: Polsia was criticized for mixing them).
- M1 / M3 retention, Time to First Result, % of companies with revenue.
- Customer revenue processed (proof the product works).
- Cost per active company and gross margin.
- Market comp: Polsia raised $30M at ~$250M (May 2026) on single-digit-millions recurring revenue.

### How the agents run it
- Investor research agent: finds investors who backed AI-agent, solo-founder and SMB-tool companies; builds a CRM.
- Monthly investor update (also public as a newsletter), written by the agents from live metrics.
- Personalized outreach and a data room that updates itself from live metrics.
- The founder takes the calls and signs. Everything else is prepared by agents. That is itself a build-in-public story.
