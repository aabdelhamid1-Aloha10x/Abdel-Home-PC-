# Masar Solo TV ad treatment (draft, 2026-10-14)

## What the trend research says (vidIQ, YouTube Shorts, last 6 months)
- "Owner Operator Weekly Pay Breakdown: $7,650 Gross, $4,290 Kept" - 530K views, still ~545 views/hour. Gross vs kept is the hottest frame in the niche. This is exactly our "revenue vs kept" message.
- "Goodbye company driver, hello owner operator" - 405K views, ~1,900 views/hour. The day-one-of-my-own-authority moment.
- "Load refused, another great day of trucking" - 706K views. Honest, a bit funny, real problems.
- "Do not become an owner operator!", "Would I become an owner-operator again?", "Lost everything, then bought another truck. Here's what he changed" - struggle and comeback stories perform.
- Diesel price posts and "this truck is not good for owner operators #money" (947K) - money talk wins.
- Search: "owner operator" ~4.8K/month (US), "truck dispatching" +77% in 30 days, "truckers coach" +30%, "business owner" +50%.

## Production stack (tools we already have connected)
- Cinematic footage: ElevenLabs Flows video models (Google Veo 3.1, Seedance 2.5, Kling, Runway Gen-4.5) or Higgsfield (Seedance 2.5, Kling 3.0). 1080p, 16:9 master plus 9:16 cut.
- Original song or score: ElevenLabs Music v2.5 with our own lyrics (we own the output; no copied songs).
- Voiceover: ElevenLabs v4 voice (deep, warm, trucker-credible), or a custom designed voice.
- Assembly: ElevenLabs Composition node (video timeline + music + voiceover + sound effects).
- App screens: real screen recordings of the Solo v3 app (demo data) inserted as phone shots. AI video cannot draw our exact UI, so app moments are real captures or a phone-in-hand shot with our screen composited.
- Disclosure: label realistic AI-generated footage where YouTube, Meta and TikTok require it.

## Direction A: "Gross is a lie" (30 s, cinematic, voiceover)
Look: night rain on the interstate, sodium lights, slow push-ins, shallow depth of field, then dawn.
0-3 s  Cold open. Wipers. A driver's tired eyes in the mirror. VO: "Seventy-six hundred gross this week."
3-7 s  Fuel nozzle clicking, receipt printing, toll gantry flashing. VO: "So why does it feel like you're working for free?"
7-12 s Phone lights up the cab: Masar Home, kept vs revenue. VO: "Masar shows what you keep. Before you book."
12-18 s Offer screen, keep ring, counter +$300, email preview, thumb taps Send. VO: "Counter in two taps. Masar writes it. You send it."
18-24 s Sunrise, truck crests a hill. Invoice PAID. VO: "Chase the late invoice. Log the fuel. Just talk."
24-30 s Hero shot, logo. VO: "Masar Solo. Your back office, on autopilot. Fourteen days free. Cancel anytime."

## Direction B: the song "Keep What You Make" (30 s and 60 s)
Style: outlaw-country groove with modern 808 drums, slide guitar, gang vocals on the hook. Hype, not cheesy.
Verse 1:
  Ten-four at four a.m., coffee in the cup holder,
  Diesel climbing every week, these miles getting colder.
  Broker says "six months, son," I'm parked and I'm waiting,
  Gross looks good on paper but the bills keep stacking.

  So I ask before I book it, what's it really leave me?
Hook (gang vocals):
  Keep what you make, keep what you make,
  Every mile, every load, know the number you take.
  Tap it, talk it, send it, roll,
  Masar's got the office, I got the road.
Verse 2 (60 s cut):
  Late invoice? Masar nudged it. Running late? One tap.
  Break-even on the dash, I don't guess, I don't gamble that.
Tag: "Masar Solo. Fourteen days free."

## Direction C: "Day one" (new authority, 30 s)
Story: company driver hands back the keys, first morning in his own truck, doubt, then Masar as the co-driver.
Hold: needs the new-authority filter and verified broker data before it can claim anything about loads for new MCs.

## Claims guardrails
Numbers are examples and labelled. No promised earnings. No credits mentioned. Every outward action shows the driver tapping Send. Trial: 14 days, card required, cancel anytime.
