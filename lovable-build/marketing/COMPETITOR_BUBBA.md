# Competitor research: Hey Bubba Inc ("Bubba AI", bubba.ai / heybubba.ai)
Researched 2026-10-17 from public web sources only (no login, no contact with the company). Company-reported numbers are marked CLAIM. Independent numbers are marked INDEP. Gaps are marked UNKNOWN.

## 1. Who they are
- Hey Bubba Inc, Austin TX (per press listings). Product "Bubba AI", voice AI dispatcher for owner-operators and small fleets. Sites: bubba.ai (main) and heybubba.ai. Phone 775-922-4050.
- Founder/CEO Tapan Chaudhari, who previously founded TruckX (ELD/fleet management, Sunnyvale CA; directory listings claim 10K+ small fleets, about $21.7M est. revenue, about 121 staff: unverified aggregator data). Co-founders per their About page: Clarissa Luttmann (CPO), Aswin Chandrasekaran (CTO). Also Gabriel Ribeiro (Head of Partnerships & Marketing), Drew Karpacz (VP Industry Relations), Jeff Resch (Senior Fleet AI Strategist). One article says co-founded May 2025; launch was March 25, 2025, so treat founding date as UNKNOWN.
- Funding: Overdrive (per search summary; page itself blocked for me) says the founder self-funded about $3M, no outside investors, and no one had paid yet. Tracxn lists no funding. Crunchbase page blocked. Treat as: no known outside funding (unconfirmed).

## 2. Product
- Voice-first mobile app (iOS "Hey Bubba AI", Android com.heybubba) with wake word. Searches load boards you already pay for (DAT, Truckstop, Uber Freight, Parade, LoadSurf listed), calls/emails/texts brokers, negotiates, books, files rate cons and PODs, fuel receipt capture for IFTA, broker credit/days-to-pay visibility, reload/backhaul search, multilingual (about 30-40 languages per FreightWaves quote). Uses Motive and Samsara telematics/HOS data (marketplace listings).
- Control: bring every load for approval, or auto-book within limits (lanes, equipment, rate floor). Escalates to a human below floor / odd terms / breakdown.
- "Autopilot" mode was a roadmap item at launch (2025); their site now sells "AI AutoPilot". Rate negotiation automation went to beta users in May 2025.
- Fleet side: "ActionWeave AI" (recently launched agent-orchestration backend; agents for accounts receivable, compliance, booking). FreightWaves Sept 28 2026: negotiates with 5-10 brokers at once and returns ranked offers; enterprise customers "piloting"; Ribeiro says carriers under 50-100 trucks are still early in AI adoption. Podcast (July 14 2026): positioned as an "AI brain" on top of TMS, email, phone, telematics, load boards.
- TMS: I found NO TMS product. They sit on top of other TMS platforms (integrations incl. Parade and others). "Enterprise" on their site = custom plan + free pilot, no feature detail. Their site has no screenshots, product videos or demo footage on the pages I could read (owner-operator page: no testimonials, counts, videos or screenshots). The homepage has a mic demo button and three short owner-operator quotes.
- Security claims on site: SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, GDPR, CCPA (CLAIM, not verified).

## 3. Pricing
- Beta: free, unlimited search/negotiate/book. Launch promo (Mar 2025): 3 months free with a DOT-registered email.
- At general availability (owner-operator page): first 5 booked loads free, then 2.99% of the negotiated rate per booked load, no subscription/seat/minimum. A blog post for fleets says 60-day trial then 2.5% on loads booked through Bubba with volume discounts. Back-office automation priced separately by contact; enterprise custom with free pilot.
- Overdrive: no one had paid as of its article; charging planned "next quarter" (date of article unconfirmed).

## 6. Correction to the working assumption
Impression "nothing in the past year except a couple of videos and one trade show" is not supported: public record shows continuous app releases (17 in about 13 months), new fleet product (ActionWeave), several 2026 articles/podcasts (Jul 14, Aug 27, Sep 28 2026), association partnerships, and integrations. What is true: no public pricing in effect yet (still free beta per their site), no TMS, little visual product proof on the site, no independent user reviews, and tiny App Store review count.

## 7. Implications for Masar (to use in the marketing plan)
- They are a voice dispatcher layer on top of other systems; Masar is the system of record (TMS, accounting, maintenance, safety, driver app) with the AI inside. Lead with "whole back office, not just load booking".
- They are free/pay-per-booked-load (about 2.5-3%); Masar is subscription. Expect "why pay monthly" objections; counter with what a per-load fee grows into on a busy fleet and with everything Bubba doesn't do (invoicing/settlements, maintenance, safety, chat, drivers).
- Their weak spots: no visual proof, no reviews, no TMS, company-reported numbers only, broker-negotiation-first. Strong spots: head start (launched Mar 2025), TruckX brand and customer base to cross-sell, trade press, load board/telematics marketplace listings, association channels.
- Do not copy their unverifiable claims (user counts, certifications). Our claims must be provable.
- Marketing plan inputs still needed: their social follower counts and traffic (manual lookup), Google Play installs, their App Store screenshots/video, TruckX customer overlap.
