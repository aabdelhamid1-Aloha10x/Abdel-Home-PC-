Chart rules

  • One question per chart, goal line always visible.
  • Plain SVG, so it draws the same in the iOS and Android WebView.
  • Bars grow from zero in 360 ms. Late invoices shown in amber then coral, never red-only.
  • Every figure carries a confidence badge: Estimated, Learning, or Based on your data.
  • Tap a bar to open that week's loads.

7. Rules so it behaves inside the Capacitor app

What each screen above obeys, so nothing janks on a mid-range phone.

Motion

  • Transform and opacity only. No animating width, height or blur.
  • Durations 90, 140, 220, 360, 700 ms. One spring: stiffness 420, damping 34.
  • Reduced motion turns every animation into an instant state change.

Layout

  • Safe-area padding on the top bar, composer and tab bar.
  • Tap targets at least 44 px. Composer lifts above the keyboard.
  • Charts are SVG, never canvas libraries that fight the WebView.

Native feel

  • Haptics through one wrapper: light on tap, success on Approve, warning on Stop.
  • Voice notes use the native recorder. Photos use the native camera.
  • Dark and light follow the phone, with the same lime accent.
