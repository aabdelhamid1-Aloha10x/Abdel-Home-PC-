Mockups: `Site*.dc.html` in `ui_v2/project/` (generated by `gen11.py`). Brand name is a placeholder in ONE constant (`BRAND`, in the app `src/config/brand.ts`). Money is record only: the app never moves money. No per-action credit prices anywhere; only plan prices and credit-pack prices.

## 2. Pages: purpose and every button
**SiteHome / SiteHomeMobile** Purpose: explain the product, get a trial start. Sticky nav: Product scrolls to features, Pricing, For owner-operators, For fleets, Sign in, Start free trial (sets plan Core, opens sign-up), Light/Dark/Auto theme. Hero planner card: Approve changes the suggested bar to assigned, Not now marks skipped, Undo restores. Audience cards open Solo or Fleets. Assistant-mode tabs (Suggest, Auto with limits, Off) swap the explanation. Pricing teaser buttons set the plan and open sign-up; Enterprise opens demo. FAQ accordion: one open at a time. Footer opens SiteLegal on the chosen tab. Mobile: menu button opens a stacked menu.
**SitePricing** Monthly/Annual toggle changes all prices (annual = pay 10 months, get 12: Solo $390, Core $970, Growth $2,490 a year). Plan buttons set `site_plan` and open sign-up; Enterprise opens demo. Credit pack cards select/unselect ($25 for 1,000; $110 for 5,000; $400 for 20,000; never expire) and show a Start free trial button. Comparison table is read only. FAQ accordion. Talk to sales opens demo.
**SiteSolo / SiteFleets** Benefit pages. Solo phone card: Send counter / Undo. Fleets: truck-count buttons (1 to 3, 4 to 25, 26+) show Core, Growth or Enterprise card. CTAs as Home. FAQ accordions.
**SiteDemo** Lead form for Growth and Enterprise. Fields: name, company, MC or DOT, work email, phone, trucks (1-3, 4-10, 11-25, 26-50, 50+), tools used today (multi), best time (morning, afternoon, any), hidden honeypot. Request a demo: invalid shows inline errors; valid calls `submit-lead` and shows the thank-you state (Start free trial, Back to home, Send another). The spec note card on the page is not shown to visitors.
**SiteSignUp** Plan cards (Solo, Core, Growth; Enterprise swaps the form for a Talk to sales panel). Google, Apple, Phone on one row, then email, name, password (min 10, Show/Hide), Terms checkbox. Trial terms side card: 14 days, no card needed, email on day 12, account pauses (data kept 30 days) if nothing happens. Create account: errors inline, else creates the user and opens verify.
**SiteVerify** Email code or text message toggle, 6-digit code, expires in 10 minutes. Verify continues to welcome; wrong code shows an error; Send a new code (rate limited); change email or number returns to sign-up.
**SiteWelcome** Trial has started and ends in 14 days. Three tiles: Start setup (onboarding), Get the app, Add payment (optional). Solo tile text changes.
**SiteCheckout** Optional card during trial. Plan summary with monthly/annual, promo code (`SAMPLE10` in the mockup only), optional credit pack, Stripe hosted page placeholder (card never touches our servers), order summary with Due today and First plan charge date, tax note (Stripe Tax, only where it applies), receipts note. Continue to Stripe opens Stripe Checkout in setup mode; return shows Payment method saved.
**SiteOnboard** Nine steps with stepper, Back / Next / Skip, validation, progress saved (mockup: localStorage; real: `companies.onboarding` jsonb). Plan-aware: Solo skips How you work and Invite and ends on SiteGetApp; Core hides Teams; Growth shows teams and the setup-call card. Steps: 1 Company (MC/DOT lookup, FMCSA card with authority, insurance-on-file note, address prefill, Not you? manual entry), 2 Fleet and freight (trucks, trailers, equipment, haul type, lanes, plan limit notice), 3 How you work (just me / dispatcher and drivers / team, teams yes/no, programs, pay methods), 4 Invite (up to 5 email+role rows, driver text invite, QR, copy link), 5 Bring your data (TMS CSV, QuickBooks export, spreadsheet, start empty, sample company), 6 Connect (Samsara, Motive, Geotab, QuickBooks, fuel card, texting number; each Connect or Skip, with Undo; status chip Ready / Needs approval / Registration needed), 7 Cost settings (cost per mile, fuel, mpg, live profit example), 8 Assistants (Off / Suggest / Auto per assistant, default Suggest, All Suggest, All Off), 9 Done (first-week checklist with deep links, progress bar, Growth: book a 20-minute setup call).
**SiteGetApp** Solo and Driver tabs, App Store and Google Play buttons (links from brand config), QR, Use it in your browser, Back to setup checklist.
**SiteSignIn** Google, Apple, Phone row, email and password, Forgot password (reset form, sent notice), link to sign-up. Valid sign-in opens the app.
**SiteLegal** Tabs: Terms, Privacy, Security, Contact (form with validation and sent state), Status (read-only list from a status provider).
**SiteClickMap** Table of every control per screen.

## 3. Leads
Table `leads`: id, created_at, source (demo_form, contact_form, signup_abandoned, pricing_enterprise), plan_interest (solo, core, growth, enterprise), name, company, mc_dot, trucks_band, email, phone, current_tools text[], preferred_time, stage (new, contacted, demo, trial, won, lost), owner (user id), notes, utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content, referrer, landing_path, consent_at, ip_hash, converted_company_id.
Flow: browser calls edge function `submit-lead` only (no direct table access). It checks the honeypot (non-empty means pretend success, store nothing), rate limit (5 per IP hash per hour), validates with zod, inserts with the service role, emails sales, auto-replies to the visitor. RLS on `leads`: no public policies; staff read/update by role. Sign-up links a lead to a tenant by email: stage becomes trial, then won when the first paid invoice posts.

## 4. Lead to tenant funnel (events in order)
visit, signup_start, signup_verified, company_created, onboarding_step_completed (step 1 to 9), first_load_created, first_invoice_sent, subscription_started (paid). Report each as a count and a step-to-step rate in an admin view. Trial start is `signup_verified`.

## 5. Email sequences (from `hello@brand-domain`, plain text, reply goes to a person)
**Welcome (on verify)** Subject: You are in. Body: "Hi {name}, your 14-day trial of {plan} has started and ends on {date}. No card was needed. Start here: {setup link}. If you get stuck, reply to this email and a person answers."
**Day 1** Subject: Two things to do today. Body: "Hi {name}, two quick wins: add your first load (or forward a rate con) and connect your ELD. Both take about 5 minutes. {checklist link}"
**Day 3 nudge (if setup under 50 percent)** Subject: Want a hand with setup? Body: "Hi {name}, you are {pct} through setup. The most common stuck point is importing data. Reply with what you use today and we will tell you the fastest way. {book a call link on Growth}"
**Trial ending, day 12** Subject: Your trial ends in 2 days. Body: "Hi {name}, your trial ends on {date}. To keep going, add a payment method: {checkout link}. If you do nothing, your account pauses and your data is kept for 30 days. No charge happens without a card."
**Win-back (30 days after pause)** Subject: Still need a hand with dispatch? Body: "Hi {name}, your data is still here until {date}. If something was missing, tell us what and we will say honestly if we can fix it. Reopen: {link}."
Every email has an unsubscribe link for non-account emails and the company postal address.

## 7. SEO
Titles under 60 chars: Home "Trucking back office software | {BRAND}"; Pricing "Pricing for trucking companies | {BRAND}"; Owner-operators "Software for owner-operators | {BRAND}"; Fleets "Dispatch and back office for fleets | {BRAND}". Descriptions under 155 chars, plain words. One H1 per page. `/sitemap.xml` lists public routes; `robots.txt` blocks `/app`, `/onboarding`, `/sign-up/*` steps, allows the rest. Canonical URLs, Open Graph image per page. JSON-LD: Organization, SoftwareApplication with offers (Solo $39, Core $97, Growth $249, USD, monthly), FAQPage on Home, Pricing and Solo. Pages are server-rendered or prerendered so crawlers see text.

## 8. Legal pages needed
Terms of service, Privacy policy, Security overview, Cookie notice (only if non-essential cookies), Acceptable use, Data processing addendum (for fleets that ask), Subprocessor list, SMS and texting consent language for driver and applicant texts, Refund and cancellation terms, Contact and postal address. The mockup texts are placeholders; a lawyer writes the real ones.

## 9. Claims we may NOT make unless true (and proven)
No SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, PCI, FMCSA "certified" or "approved" ELD or "FMCSA partner" claims. No "bank-level" or "military-grade" security. No uptime percentage without a measured history. No customer counts, logos, quotes, star ratings or awards unless they are real and we have permission (the mockup quotes are labeled Sample). No "saves X percent" or "saves X hours" numbers without our own data. No "guaranteed compliant" or "never fail an audit". No claim that we hold or move money, or that we are a broker, factor or insurer. No "integrates with" a product that is only planned: show Ready or Needs approval honestly. No "AI does everything": assistants start on Suggest. No SSO, API or audit export until Enterprise features ship.
